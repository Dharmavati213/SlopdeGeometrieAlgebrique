## Typographical corrections

Corrected in the English and marked `% typo:` in the source: “respectivment” (deduction of 4.1 bis from 4.1);
the missing closing parenthesis before the second definition of the functor in the proof of 5.3; “un point S”
(6.1 ter); “si est seulement si” (proof of 6.2 i)); the missing “pas” in “ne soit le radical” (proof of 6.2 v));
“présentation fine” (proof of 6.3); “n'étant par nécessairement” (before 8.12).
