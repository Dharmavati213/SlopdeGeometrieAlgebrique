\chapter*{About this draft}
This volume collects the foreword, introduction and Expos\'es I--XXVI,
including the A and B parts, of \textit{Sch\'emas en groupes} (SGA 3).
The French source is the recomposition edited by P. Gille and P. Polo.
Statement numbers and references retain the printed French numbering.
Clear typographical slips corrected in the English are marked in the
LaTeX source; apparent mathematical slips are retained and recorded in
the per-expos\'e READMEs. Independent scholarly proofreading remains outstanding.
The translation contribution is licensed under the MIT License;
the French original remains copyright of its authors and publishers.
