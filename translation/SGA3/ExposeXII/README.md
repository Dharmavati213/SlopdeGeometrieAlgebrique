## Typographical corrections

Corrected in the English and marked `% typo:` in the source: ``te que'' (5.2);
a missing closing parenthesis after BIBLE 7 th. 3 a) (proof of 6.6);
``une applications'' (7.1 f)); ``par nécessairement'' (7.2); the duplicated k in
``un corps k algébriquement clos k'' (8.3); ``la groupe additif'' (8.4); a stray
quotation mark (9.1); ``un faisceaux'' (9.3); ``pour le sous le groupe'' (9.3);
a missing ``pour'' (proof of 9.4) and a missing ``que'' (proof of 9.8);
``tranformations'' (9.9).
