## Typographical corrections

Corrected in the English and marked `% typo:` in the source: mojibake in
printed French words (proofs of 4.1.1 and 4.1.3, Remark 5.1); a missing verb
(``on le diagramme'') in the proof of 6.1 (i); an unmatched opening
parenthesis in reference [Mur65].
