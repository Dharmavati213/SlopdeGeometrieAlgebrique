## Typographical corrections

Corrected in the English and marked `% typo:` in the source: *que loi* →
*que la loi* and *il en donc* → *il en est donc* (proof of 0.7.bis);
*lois de compositions* → *lois de composition* (1.3.6, step c); *une suites
exacte* → *une suite exacte* (2.0); a missing period after \(S_0\) (3.9);
*faiceaux* → *faisceaux*, twice (proof of 4.8); a missing space in
*relevant \(Y_{\mathcal{J}}\),considérons* (4.17); \(\partial^{1}d((Y,Y')\)
→ \(\partial^{1}d(Y,Y')\) (4.20); an extra closing parenthesis after each of
two displays (4.22); *cocyle* → *cocycle* (4.38, 3).
