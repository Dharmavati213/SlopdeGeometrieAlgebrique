## Typographical corrections

Corrected in the English and marked `% typo:` in the source: “un en un seul” for “un et un seul” (1.5 d), p. 3);
punctuation after “corps” (proof of 2.1, p. 3); duplicated “de” in 2.11 a) and “section et” for “section est”
(2.11, p. 6); “sous-akgèbres” (3.2, p. 12); “il n'existe par” (3.17 b), p. 16); “extension transcendante pur”
(proof of 6.1, p. 26); “de Lie de de T” (appendix, p. 33).
