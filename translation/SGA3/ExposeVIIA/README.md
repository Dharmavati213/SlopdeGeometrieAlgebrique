## Typographical corrections

Corrected in the English and marked `% typo:` in the source: the redundant
“de” before the element in 3.2.3; the singular “la categories” in the proof
of 8.1.3; the omitted verb in “Comme G → S affine” in 8.5.2; “Groups
schemes” in the Tate–Oort bibliography title.
