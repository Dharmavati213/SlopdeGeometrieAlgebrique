## Typographical corrections

Corrected in the English and marked `% typo:` in the source: the missing
“de” in “un corps caractéristique” (1.5); “un-k-groupe” (3.9 ter); “corps de
classeq” in Serre's title (proof of 3.9 ii)); the missing adjective in “le plus
entier” in the starred note to the proof of 4.2.1 (rendered “the least
integer”); “de base H est de groupe” (proof of 5.4.1, rendered “with base H and
group”); a missing closing parenthesis after a parenthetical remark (end of
the proof of 5.1.1 ii) b)); “(1970.” → “(1970).” in an editorial note (proof
of 6.2.2); the missing closing parenthesis in the reference to X 4.8 b)
(proof of 7.1.1).
