## Typographical corrections

Corrected in the English and marked `% typo:` in the source: ``tels'' (for
``tel'', referring to the neighborhood) in the proof of 2.1.3; ``Dautre'' in
3.2.5; ``la translations'' in the proof of 6.1; ``Mah.'' for ``Math.'' in
reference [Per76].
