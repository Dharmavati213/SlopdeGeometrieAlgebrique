## Typographical corrections

Corrected in the English and marked `% typo:` in the source: the missing closing
parenthesis in 5.10.3 (d); the omitted “ce” in “D’après ce qui précède” in the
proof of 5.9.3; “mutiplicatif” for “multiplicatif” in editorial note 63.
