\section{Descent of \'etale preschemes: effectiveness
criteria}
\label{IX.4}

\begin{proposition}
\label{IX.4.1}
Let $g\colon S'\to S$ be a faithfully flat and quasi-compact
morphism. Then~$g$ is an effective descent morphism for
the fibered category of preschemes \'etale,
separated and of finite type over others.
\end{proposition}

It is indeed a descent morphism for the fibered category
in question, by virtue of~\Ref{IX.3.3} or of
VIII~\Ref{VIII.5.2}, as one prefers. It remains to show that if~$X'$ is
\'etale, separated and of finite type over~$S'$, and endowed with a
descent datum relative to $g\colon S'\to S$, the
latter is effective in the fibered category in
question. Now one easily sees that if~$X$ is a prescheme
over~$S$, then
% original p. 236
it is \'etale over~$S$ if and only if it is \'etale over~$S'$
(by virtue of definition~\Ref{IX.1.1} and of \loccit \Ref{VIII.3.6}). Hence it is \'etale, separated and of
finite type over~$S$ if and only if~$X'$ is so over~$S'$, \cf for
example~\Ref{IX.2.4}. Hence it suffices to make sure of the
effectiveness of the descent datum on~$X$ for the
fibered category of arrows of~$\Sch$. Now~$X'$ is
quasi-affine over~$S'$ by virtue of VIII~\Ref{VIII.6.2}
and~\Ref{VIII.6.6}. One can then conclude by using
VIII~\Ref{VIII.7.9}. The reader will note moreover that the
proof requires less if one restricts oneself to preschemes
\'etale and \emph{finite} over others, for one can then invoke
VIII~\Ref{VIII.2.1} directly.

\begin{corollary}
\label{IX.4.2}
Let $g\colon S'\to S$ be a universally submersive morphism, $X'$
an
$S'$-prescheme
\'etale separated and of finite type, endowed with a
descent datum relative to~$g$, $S_1\to S$ a faithfully
flat and quasi-compact morphism, $S_1'$ and~$X_1'$ deduced
from~$S'$ and~$X'$ by change of base, so that $S_1'\to S_1$
is universally submersive, $X_1'$ is \'etale separated and
of finite type over~$S_1'$, and endowed with a descent datum
relative to $g_1\colon S_1'\to S_1$. For the
descent datum on~$X'$ to be effective, it is necessary and sufficient that the
descent datum on~$X_1'$ be so.
\end{corollary}

This follows from the theory of descent in
categories \cite{IX.D}, taking into account~\Ref{IX.4.1} and~\Ref{IX.3.3}.

One proves in an analogous way:

\begin{corollary}
\label{IX.4.3}
Let $g\colon S'\to S$ be a universally submersive morphism, $X'$
an \'etale~$S'$-prescheme endowed with a descent datum
relative to~$g$, $(S_i)$ a covering of~$S$ by
open sets. For the descent datum to be effective, it is necessary
and sufficient that for every~$i$, the corresponding descent
datum on~$X_i'=X\times_S S_i$, relative to the morphism
$g_i\colon S_i'=S'\times_S S_i\to S_i$, be so.
\end{corollary}

This last result leads to bringing out a local effectiveness
criterion:

\begin{proposition}
\label{IX.4.4}
Let $g\colon S'\to S$ be a morphism of finite presentation
\textup{VIII}~\Ref{VIII.3.6}
% original p. 237
and universally submersive, $X'$ a prescheme \'etale and
of finite presentation over~$S'$, endowed with a descent datum
relative to~$g$, and finally~$a$ a point of~$S$. For there to exist
an open neighborhood~$U$ of~$a$ such that the corresponding descent
datum on~$X_U'=X'\times_S U$ relative to the morphism
$$g_U\colon S_U'=S'\times_S U\to S_U=U$$ is effective, it is necessary and
sufficient that the corresponding descent datum on $X_a'=X'\times_S\Spec(\cal{O}_a)$, relative to the morphism
$$g_a\colon S_a'=S'\times_S\Spec(\cal{O}_a)\to S_a=\Spec(\cal{O}_a),$$
be effective.
\end{proposition}

The necessity being trivial, let us show the sufficiency. We
therefore have an \'etale prescheme of finite type~$X_a$
over~$S_a$, and an isomorphism
\begin{equation*}
\label{eq:IX.4.*}
\tag{$*$} {X_a'\isomto X_a\times_{S_a}S_a'}
\end{equation*}
compatible with the descent data. In accordance with
an easy general sorites on preschemes
defined over an inductive limit of rings (here the rings~$A_f$,
where~$A$ is the ring of an affine open neighborhood of~$a$, and
where~$f$ runs through the elements of~$A$ that are not in
the prime ideal corresponding to~$a$), one can find an
open neighborhood~$U$ of~$a$, an \'etale prescheme of finite
type~$X_U$ over~$U=S_U$, and an $S_a$-isomorphism $X_a\isomto
X_U\times_{S_U}S_a$. Moreover, taking~$U$ small enough, one can then
assume that the isomorphism~\eqref{eq:IX.4.*} comes from an
isomorphism:
$$
X_U'\isomto X_U\times_{S_U} S_U';
$$
the latter might not be compatible with the descent
data; however, provided one shrinks~$U$, it will be
compatible with the descent data. This completes the
proof.

\begin{corollary}
\label{IX.4.5}
Under the conditions of~\Ref{IX.4.4}, for the descent
datum on~$X'$ to be effective, it is necessary and sufficient that for every
$a\in S$, the corresponding descent datum on~$X_a'$,
relative to the morphism
$S_a'=S'\times_S\Spec(\cal{O}_a)\to\Spec(\cal{O}_a)$, be so.
When~$S$ is locally noetherian, and~$X'$ separated
over~$S'$, one can in the preceding criterion also replace
$\cal{O}_a$ by its completion.
\end{corollary}

The
% original p. 238
first assertion follows from~\Ref{IX.4.4} and~\Ref{IX.4.3}, the
second is then a consequence of~\Ref{IX.4.2}. Using
\Ref{IX.4.2} again and the fact that for every noetherian local
ring~$A$, one can find a complete noetherian local
ring~$B$ and a local homomorphism $A\to B$ such that~$B$ is flat
over~$A$ and $B/\goth{m}B$ is a given extension of the residue
field $k=A/\goth{m}$ of~$A$, one finds:

\begin{corollary}
\label{IX.4.6}
Under the conditions of~\Ref{IX.4.4}, assume moreover~$X'$
separated over~$S'$, and~$S$ locally noetherian. For the
descent datum on~$X'$ to be effective, it is necessary and sufficient
that for every prescheme~$S_1$ over~$S$ which is the spectrum of a
complete local ring with algebraically closed residue field, the
corresponding descent datum on~$X_1'=X'\times_S S_1$,
relative to the morphism $g_1\colon S_1'\to S_1$, be effective.
\end{corollary}

\begin{theorem}
\label{IX.4.7}
Let $g\colon S'\to S$ be a morphism which is finite and surjective, and of
finite presentation (this last hypothesis being a
consequence of the others if~$S$ is locally
noetherian)\footnote{One can show that it in fact suffices that~$g$
be an \emph{integral} morphism, by reducing to the case of the text by a
limit procedure in the style of EGA IV~8.}.
Then~$g$ is an effective descent morphism for the fibered
category of preschemes \'etale, separated, of
finite type over others.
\end{theorem}

One must show that if~$X'$ is \'etale, separated, of
finite type over~$S'$, and endowed with a descent datum relative
to~$g$, then this datum is effective.
Using~\Ref{IX.4.3}, one easily reduces to the case where~$S$
is noetherian. Thanks to~\Ref{IX.4.5}, one can therefore
assume that~$S$ is the spectrum of a noetherian local ring, a
fortiori that
$$
\dim S=n<+\infty.
$$
One then argues by induction on~$\dim S=n$, the assertion
being trivial for $n<0$. Assume therefore $n\geq 0$ and the
theorem proved for the dimensions $n'<n$. By
virtue of~\Ref{IX.4.6} one is reduced to the case where~$S$ is the
spectrum of a complete local ring, hence~$S'$ is a finite union
of spectra of complete local rings. We therefore have
$$
X'=X_1'\sqcup X_2'
$$
where~$X_1'$ is \emph{finite} over~$S'$, and where~$X_2'$ has no
point lying over any of the closed points
% original p. 239
of~$S'$. Consider the morphisms
$$
\xymatrix@C=.5cm{ q_1,q_2\colon X'' \ar@<2pt>[r] \ar@<-2pt>[r] & X' }
$$
corresponding to the descent datum, compatible with
$\xymatrix@C=.5cm{p_1,p_2\colon S'' \ar@<2pt>[r] \ar@<-2pt>[r] & S'}$. One
sees at once that
$$
X''=q_i^{-1}(X_1')\sqcup q_i^{-1}(X_2')\quad i=1,2
$$
is the analogous canonical decomposition of~$X''$ over~$S''$, which
implies $q_1^{-1}(X_1')=\allowbreak q_2^{-1}(X_1')$ and consequently~$X_1'$ and~$X_2'$
are endowed with induced descent data. Now let~$T$ be the open set
of~$S$ complementary to its closed point, so that $T'=S'\times_S
T$ is the part of~$S'$ complementary to the set of closed
points, and~$X_2'$, which lies entirely over~$T'$,
is endowed with a descent datum relative to the morphism $T'\to
T$ induced by~$g$. As the latter is finite surjective, and as $\dim
T<\dim S=n$, this descent datum is effective by
the induction hypothesis. One therefore sees that it suffices to
prove that the descent datum on~$X_1'$ is effective, hence
one can now assume~$X'$ \'etale and \emph{finite} over~$S'$.
(N.B. the argument by induction is unnecessary if one restricts oneself
to \'etale coverings in statement~\Ref{IX.4.7}).
Let then~$S_0$ be the spectrum of the residue field of~$A$, let
$S_0'=S'\times_S S_0$, and define similarly~$S_0''$, $S_0'''$
starting from the fibered squares and cubes~$S''$ and~$S'''$
of~$S'$ over~$S$. By virtue of~\Ref{IX.1.8}, the morphisms $S_0\to S$,
$S_0'\to S'$, etc.\ induce equivalences for the
categories of \'etale coverings of~$S$ and~$S_0$ on the one
hand, $S'$ and~$S_0'$ on the other hand, etc. By the sorites of
the theory of descent in categories \cite{IX.D}, it follows
that for $g\colon S'\to S$ to be an effective descent morphism
for the fibered category of \'etale coverings, it
is necessary and sufficient that the same hold for~$g_0\colon S_0'\to S_0$.
But this is indeed the case, as a special case of~\Ref{IX.4.1}, for
example. This completes the proof.

\begin{corollary}
\label{IX.4.8}
The conclusion of~\Ref{IX.4.7} remains valid if one only assumes that
$S'\to S$ is universally submersive, of finite type and quasi-finite,
provided one assumes~$S$ locally noetherian.
\end{corollary}

By
% original p. 240
virtue of~\Ref{IX.4.6}, one can indeed assume that~$S$ is the
spectrum of a complete noetherian local ring. Then by virtue
of~\Ref{IX.2.5}, there exist a finite and surjective morphism $S_1\to S$
and an $S$-morphism $S_1\to S'$. As $S_1\to S$ is a
universal strict descent morphism for the fibered category
under consideration, by virtue of~\Ref{IX.4.7}, and as $S'\to S$ is a
universal descent morphism for the said category, \Ref{IX.4.8} follows
from the general sorites~\cite{IX.D}.

\begin{corollary}
\label{IX.4.9}
Let $g\colon S'\to S$ be a morphism of finite type, surjective and
universally open, with~$S$ locally noetherian. Then~$g$
is an effective descent morphism for the fibered
category of preschemes \'etale, separated and of
finite type over others.
\end{corollary}

Proceeding as in~\Ref{IX.4.7}, one is reduced to the case
where~$S$ is the spectrum of a noetherian and
complete local ring~$A$. Let~$A_1$ be a finite algebra over~$A$, with
spectrum~$S_1$, such that $S_1\to S$ is finite and \emph{surjective},
hence a universal effective descent morphism for the
fibered category under consideration, thanks to~\Ref{IX.4.7}.
It then follows from the general theorems \cite{IX.D}
that~$g$ is an effective descent morphism for the fibered
category under consideration if and only if the
corresponding morphism $g_1\colon S_1'=S'\times_S S_1\to S_1$ is so. As the
latter satisfies the same hypotheses as~$g$, one is
reduced to proving~\Ref{IX.4.9} for~$S_1$ instead of~$S$.
Taking first for~$A_1$ the direct product of the $A/\goth{p}_i$,
for the minimal prime ideals~$\goth{p}_i$ of~$A$, one is
reduced to the case where~$A$ is \emph{integral}. One then
shows\footnote{\Cf EGA IV~14.3.13 and~14.5.4.} that there exists an
integral subscheme~$S_1$ of~$S'$, quasi-finite over~$S$ and
dominating~$S$, passing through a point of the fiber of~$S'$ at the
closed point~$y$ of~$S$ (thanks to the fact that~$S'$ is universally
open of finite type over~$S$ local noetherian integral, and
$S_y'\neq\emptyset$). As~$A$ is complete, $S_1$ is finite over~$S$,
and as it dominates~$S$, the morphism $S_1\to S$ is surjective.
Replacing~$S$ by~$S_1$ once more, one is reduced to the
case where~$S'$ has a section over~$S$, where the statement is
trivial.

\begin{theorem}
\label{IX.4.10}
Let $g\colon S'\to S$ be a finite radicial surjective morphism, of
finite presentation (this last condition being
superfluous if~$S$ is locally noetherian\footnote{It even suffices
that~$g$ be integral, radicial surjective, as one sees by
an easy reduction to the case of the text, in the style of EGA~IV~8, \cf SGA~4
VIII~1.1.}).
% original p. 241
Then the inverse image functor induces an equivalence of the
category of \'etale preschemes over~$S$ with the
category of \'etale preschemes over~$S'$.
\end{theorem}

As the diagonal morphisms from~$S'$ into $S'\times_S S'$ and
$S'\times_S S'\times_S S'$ are surjective immersions, hence
induce, by virtue of~\Ref{IX.1.9}, equivalences of the
categories of \'etale preschemes over~$S'\times_S S'$
\resp $S'\times_S S'\times_S S'$ with the category of
\'etale preschemes over~$S'$, it follows from the sorites of
descent \cite{IX.D} that every~$X'$ \'etale over~$S'$ is endowed with one
and only one descent datum relative to $g\colon
S'\to S$. Hence~\Ref{IX.3.3} implies that the inverse image functor
by~$g$, from the category of \'etale preschemes over~$S$
into the category of \'etale preschemes over~$S'$, is
\emph{fully faithful}. It remains to show that it is
essentially surjective, \ie that every~$X'$ \'etale over~$S'$ is
isomorphic to the inverse image of an~$X$ \'etale over~$S$. The
question being evidently local on~$S$ \emph{and on}~$X'$,
one can assume $S$,~$S'$,~$X'$ affine. But then~$X'$ is
separated of finite type over~$S'$, and one can apply the
effectiveness criterion~\Ref{IX.4.7}.

\begin{corollary}
\label{IX.4.11}
The conclusion~\Ref{IX.4.9} remains valid when one replaces the hypothesis
on~$g$ by: $g$~is faithfully flat, quasi-compact and radicial.
\end{corollary}

Same proof, invoking~\Ref{IX.4.1} instead
of~\Ref{IX.4.7}.

One will note that the proof of~\Ref{IX.4.7} is
``elementary'' in that it does not use the
finiteness and comparison theorems for proper morphisms (EGA~III~3,~4,~5). The same no longer holds for the following result:

\begin{theorem}
\label{IX.4.12}
Let $g\colon S'\to S$ be a proper, surjective morphism, of
finite presentation (this last hypothesis being a
consequence of the first if~$S$ is locally
noetherian). Then~$g$ is an effective descent morphism
for the fibered category of \'etale coverings of
preschemes.
\end{theorem}

By virtue of~\Ref{IX.3.3} and of~\Ref{IX.2.2}, one is reduced to
proving that for every \'etale covering~$X'$ over~$S'$, endowed
with a descent datum relative to $g\colon S'\to S$,
this descent datum is effective. Using~\Ref{IX.4.3},
one is easily reduced
% original p. 242
to the case where~$S$ is noetherian, and using~\Ref{IX.4.6}, one
can therefore assume that~$S$ is the spectrum of a
\emph{complete} noetherian local ring~$A$. Let us introduce~$S''$ and~$S'''$ as
usual, let~$S_0$ be the spectrum of the residue field of~$A$, and
let~$S_0'$,~$S_0''$,~$S_0'''$ be deduced from~$S'$,~$S''$,~$S'''$
by the change of base $S_0\to S$, \ie the fibers
of~$S'$,~$S''$,~$S'''$ at the closed point of~$S$.
By~\Ref{IX.1.10}, the morphisms $S_0\to S$, $S_0'\to S'$,
etc.\ induce equivalences of the category of
\'etale coverings over the target scheme with the category
of \'etale coverings over the source scheme. Consequently,
$g\colon S'\to S$ is a strict descent morphism for the
fibered category of \'etale coverings of
preschemes if and only if $g_0\colon S_0'\to S_0$ is,
which is indeed the case by virtue of~\Ref{IX.4.1}. This completes the
proof of~\Ref{IX.4.12}. (In this argument, we needed
from~\Ref{IX.1.10} only the fact that the functor considered
in~\Ref{IX.1.10} is \emph{fully faithful}, which does \emph{not} use
the existence theorem for coherent sheaves
in algebraic geometry.)
