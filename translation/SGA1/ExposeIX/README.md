## Source and translation choices

Source: the corrected SMF branch (`orig = false`) of
[arXiv:math/0206203v2](https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0206203v2),
`smf_doc-math_3_01.tex`, lines 14731–16490, from the chapter
`Descente des morphismes \'etales. Application au groupe fondamental`
and `\label{IX}` up to, but not including, the chapter of Exposé X.
This covers original page markers 228–260.
The French TeX and PDF are not included in this repository.

The body fragments follow [`CONVENTIONS.md`](../../CONVENTIONS.md),
section “SGA 1 — front matter and Exposés IV, V, VIII–XIII”, and use the
shared package [`sga1-en.sty`](../sga1-en.sty). All non-index labels,
`\Ref`/`\eqref`/`\cite` keys, footnotes, and diagrams are kept. The
bibliography keeps `\begin{thebibliography}{D}{IX.7}` (numbered section
7). References to other exposés print the source's numbers:
`VIII~\Ref{VIII.4.3}` prints “VIII 4.3”, as in the SMF volume. Original
page numbers remain as `% original p. N` comments. Indexes and SMF
page-layout commands are omitted.

House rules applied here as in the other new exposés: *n°* / *numéro*
is rendered “no.” (`\No`), and *changement de base* is rendered “change
of base”, the Exposé VI term. After the reviews, “base change” was
replaced by “change of base” throughout Exposés IV, V, VIII, IX, and X.

In IX.5.8 *courbe simple* is rendered literally as “simple curve”. The
old sense “non-singular” is probably intended; compare *simple* for
“smooth” in Exposé II.

## Source points for scholarly review

These apparent issues are present in the corrected French TeX. They have
been retained in the translation, in accordance with the convention
against silently repairing the source.

| Location | Source wording or notation retained | Point to review |
| --- | --- | --- |
| IX.1.6 | “open subsets of `Γ` of `X ×_S Y`” | A stray “de”. |
| IX.2.1 | The morphism is named `g` | The rest of the definition uses `f`. |
| IX.2.5, statement and proof | `S'' → S'` | `S'' → S` apparently. |
| After IX.3.1 | “morphism induced by `pr_1` from `Γ` into `Y`”; “`pr_1: Z' → X'` radicial and surjective” | `X` is meant; the second statement is about `Γ'`, not `Z'`. |
| Proof of IX.3.4 | Coincidence subprescheme “of `S''`”, “diagonal in `S''`” | These should lie in `X''`. |
| Proof of IX.4.1 | “`X` is étale over `S` iff étale over `S'`”; “effectiveness of the descent datum on `X`” | `X' = X ×_S S'` in the second clause; `X'` in the second phrase. |
| IX.4.3 | `X'_i = X ×_S S_i` | `X' ×_S S_i` is meant. |
| Proof of IX.4.7 | “residue field of `A`” | `A` is never introduced. |
| IX.4.11 | “The conclusion of IX.4.9 still holds” (key `IX.4.9` kept) | Looks like a slip for IX.4.10. |
| IX.5, preamble | `π_0(K) = 0` for connectedness; the equivalence relation generated by `(q_1, q_2)` called “transitive” | One point is expected; the wording is odd. |
| IX.5, path classes | `F''_{s''} ∘ p_1^* ≅ F'_{s'}` | `F'_{q_1(s'')}` is expected. |
| IX.5, displayed classes | `F'''_{s'''} ∘ p_{ij}^* ≅ F'''_{q_{ij}(s''')}` | The right-hand side should be an `F''`. |
| IX.5, after b) | “identified with one another the `X_{s'}`” | The prime is dropped (`X'_{s'}`). |
| IX.5 and IX.5.1 | The morphism is `g` in the text but `p` in the diagrams, in `p^*`, and in `p(\underline s_0')`; `F_0'` | Inconsistent notation; `F_0'` is undefined. |
| IX.5.8 | “category of étale coverings of `S'`”; “`S'_g` of coincidences of `id_S` and `g` of `S`” | `S` in the first; `id_{S'}` and `S'` in the second. |
| IX.5.8 | “`g = e` for `g ∈ I`”, “fixed by `I`”; “for every `g ∈ G`”; “`n`-th symmetric power of `S`”; genus `g` | `I` is undefined (`Γ`); `Γ` is meant; `X` is meant; the genus `g` is never introduced. |
| IX.6.1 and its proof | `\overline{X}`; `π_1(\overline X_0, a)` | Undefined (probably `\overline X_0`); the point should be `\overline a`. |
| Proof of IX.6.1 | `b'` | Undefined. |
| IX.6.2 | `\overline{X'}_0` next to `\overline X_0'` | Inconsistent notation. |
| Proof of IX.6.6 | Ring `A`; `X' → X ×_S S'` called a “canonical homomorphism” | `A` is undefined; the map is a morphism. |
| IX.6.7 | `X'_{s'}` | Presumably `X'_s`. |
| IX.6.9 | “`Y` locally noetherian” | `Y` is undefined (presumably `S`). |
| IX.6.10, footnote | “`g` being only universally open”; generic fiber given as `P^1_k` | `g` is undefined (presumably `f`); the generic fiber is a conic over the fraction field. |
| IX.6.11 | “geometric point `a_s` of `S_s`” | Presumably of `X_s`. |
| IX.6.12 | “`S'_{s'}` geometrically trivial over `X_s`”; “if `X'` is proper over `X`” | Presumably `X'_s`; the second repeats an assumption of IX.6.7. |
| IX.6.12, French slip | “si `S` et un préschéma de Jacobson” | *et* for *est*; no effect on the English. |

The translator also queried IX.4.12 (“this last hypothesis being a
consequence of the first”). The second review judged it correct: finite
presentation follows from properness over a locally noetherian `S`.

## Validation

The local checker `source/SGA1/check_chunk.py`, which is not in the
repository, finds for each chunk the same non-index labels, reference
and citation keys, footnotes, diagrams, displayed formulas, list items,
and statement environments as in the corrected French. For the whole
exposé these are 67 labels, 139 references, 6 citations, 9 footnotes,
8 diagrams, and 78 displays. The wrapper compiles to a 21-page PDF with
no errors or undefined references.

A second reviewer checked all four chunks against the French:

- en-1.tex: three fixes (two renderings of *numéro* as “no.”, and a
  restored second “if” after IX.3.1);
- en-3.tex: three fixes to ordinals (“nth”);
- en-2.tex and en-4.tex: no fixes.

The reviewer confirmed the translator's source points, added those on
the proof of IX.6.1 (`b'`) and IX.6.12 (*et*/*est*), and settled IX.4.12
as above. These checks do not settle the mathematical questions above;
scholarly proofreading remains outstanding.
