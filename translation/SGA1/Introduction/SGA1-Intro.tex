\chapter*{About this draft}
This is an unofficial English translation of the front matter of
A.\ Grothendieck and M.\ Raynaud,
\textit{Rev\^etements \'etales et groupe fondamental} (SGA~1),
S\'eminaire de g\'eom\'etrie alg\'ebrique du Bois Marie, 1960--61:
the abstract, the preface of the SMF edition, Grothendieck's
Introduction (Massy, August 1970), and the Foreword
(\emph{Avertissement}, Bures, June 1963) to the mimeographed notes.
It follows the slightly corrected SMF recomposition,
\href{https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0206203v2}{arXiv:math/0206203v2}.
The exposés themselves are in the sibling directories
\texttt{ExposeI}, \dots, \texttt{ExposeXIII}. Scholarly proofreading
remains outstanding.
