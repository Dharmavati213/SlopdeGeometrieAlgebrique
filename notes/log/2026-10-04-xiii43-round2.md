## What was hard, and why

1. **Galois-ness of the function field.** The tameness at `ξ` needs `K(Z)/K(X)` Galois of degree
   `n`. The SGA proof gets this for free; in Lean it was the expensive translation. What worked:
   - Push `Z` through `FEt(U) → FEt(Spec Γ(chart)) ≃ (FiniteEtale Γ)ᵒᵖ`. The base change to
     `Spec Γ(U, V)` is `Spec Γ(Z, Z⁻¹V)` via `IsOpenImmersion.isPullback` and
     `IsAffineOpen.SpecMap_appLE_fromSpec`.
   - It is connected because `Γ(Z, Z⁻¹V)` is a domain: `Z` is smooth over `V`, hence regular, hence
     integral.
   - The functor is compatible with fibre functors (`FEt.fiberSpecIso`, `FEt.pullbackFiberIso`), so
     `isGalois_obj_of_isConnected` applies.
   - The degree is then `|Hom_A(B, Ω)| = |Aut B|` (`evaluationEquivOfIsGalois`,
     `ExposeXIII.autOpEquivAlgEquiv`). Base change gives at least `n` automorphisms of `K ⊗ B`.
2. **Elaboration timeouts** cost about half the round. Any unification between a canonical
   `pullback f g` (or `W.left` of a `FEt.pullback` object, or an open subscheme of either) and
   another scheme unfolds the limit construction.
   - Symptoms: `whnf`/`isDefEq` timeouts in `rw`, `obtain … := ⟨_, rfl⟩`, `change`, and in instance
     search once several `Algebra Γ(_, _) Γ(_, _)` local instances are present.
   - Fix: the chart computation is a separate lemma, `isEtaleAt_chart` (private), stated for
     abstract `IsPullback` squares with fvar schemes, and called with `IsPullback.of_hasPullback`.
     That needed the `IsPullback` version of `CohomologyAux.isPushout_app_pullback_snd`.
   - Also: avoid `let`s mentioning such schemes in goals you `rw` (`kabstract` unfolds them). Prove
     unit computations as `.trans` chains of `map_mul`/`map_pow`.
   - Details are in `notes/topics/strategy.md` (Lean technique) and `hard-parts.md` §5.
3. **The pushout identification** `Γ(X_n, pr⁻¹V') = Γ(X, V') ⊗_V V_n`: `isPushout_app_of_isPullback`
   with `IsPullback.of_iso` across `ΓSpecIso`, then `CommRingCat.isPushout_iff_isPushout`. The
   covering chart `Γ(W, …)` is generated over `A` by `Γ(Z, …)`. To show this: it is a pushout over
   `A[1/T]`, and `T⁻¹ = T^{n-1} π⁻¹` with `π⁻¹` coming from `Z`.

## Left for `TameLiftingDVRStatement` (round 3, in this order)

Use `factorsPrimeTo_of_forall_isGalois` (TameLiftingReduction) with `s = Spec Ω₁ → Spec R`. For `Z`
Galois over `X_{Ω₁}` with `|F(Z)|` prime to `p`, the steps are:

1. **X.1.8.** With `K = Frac R` and `K̄ ⊆ Ω₁` (`IsAlgClosed.lift`), `Z ≅` the pullback of some `Z̄`
   over `X_{K̄}` (`baseChangeAlgClosedStatement`, after an iso of pullbacks). `Z̄` is Galois: it is
   connected, `|Aut Z̄| = |Aut Z| = |F|`, and the free action gives transitivity.
2. **Limit descent.** `ExposeV.isLimitBaseChangeCone` (FundamentalGroupLimit.lean) and
   `Scheme.exists_isPullback_of_isLimit_of_isFinite_of_etale` give `Z_L` over `X_L`, `L/K` finite.
   - Descend the `|F|` automorphisms with `Scheme.exists_hom_of_isPullback` at a common level.
     They stay distinct, and endomorphisms of a connected object are automorphisms, so `Z_L` is
     Galois.
   - Then pass to the separable closure `K'` of `K` in `L` by IX.4.10 (template:
     `ExposeIX.exists_mono_pullback_geometricFiberι_of_lift`).
3. **Normalization.** Take `V' = integralClosure R K'` (`TameLiftingNormalization`). The generic
   fibre of `X_{V'}` is the open `X_{V'}[1/π']`; identify it with `pullback f (Spec K' → Spec R)`.
4. **Extension.** `exists_iso_pullback_of_isGalois_of_isAdicComplete` over `V'` gives `E'` on
   `X_{V'}`. Then `isEquivalence_pullback_baseChange_of_smooth R V'` gives `E` on `X`.
5. **Chase back.** Embed `Frac V'_n` in `K̄` over `K'` and chase isomorphisms back to `X_{Ω₁}`.
   Expect most of the cost to be FEt pullback bookkeeping (`pullbackComp`, `pullbackCongr`). Keep
   the schemes abstract (lesson 2).
