# C6 started: semialgebraic sets, and Hörmander's lemma (the core of Tarski–Seidenberg) is proved

New files in `lean/SGA/Foundations/Semialgebraic/` (all build, axioms clean, mathlib style, no SGA
numbers; no existing file touched):

- `Basic.lean`: `semialgebraicSets ι : BooleanSubalgebra (Set (ι → ℝ))` generated by `{0 < p}`,
  `IsSemialgebraic`, closure lemmas, `IsSemialgebraic.induction`, `preimage_polynomialMap`.
  Using mathlib's `BooleanSubalgebra.closure` gave the Boolean closure for free.
- `OrderIso.lean`: `Real.exists_orderIso_eqOn` — a strictly monotone map on a finite subset of ℝ
  extends to `ℝ ≃o ℝ` (piecewise affine bumps + `Finset.induction_on_max`).
- `SignDiagram.lean`: `Polynomial.SignEquiv f g := ∃ h : ℝ ≃o ℝ, sign (g i (h x)) = sign (f i x)`.
  This replaces Hörmander's "sign tables" (lists) by an order automorphism; no list combinatorics
  is needed anywhere. Plus sign facts: constant sign on preconnected sets, signs at `±∞`, Rolle.
- `Hormander.lean`: `Polynomial.exists_orderIso_signs_of_hormander`: if `h` matches the signs of
  `f`, `p'` with `g`, `q'`, and of `p`, `q` on the roots of the nonzero `fᵢ` and of `p'`, and the
  signs at `±∞` agree, then some `H : ℝ ≃o ℝ` matches the signs of `f, p` with `g, q`. ≈350 lines.
- `PseudoDivision.lean`: `Polynomial.prem` with `exists_prem_eq` (`lc(Q)ᵐ P = q Q + prem P Q`) and
  `natDegree_prem_lt` (mathlib has no pseudo-division).

What was hard: nothing conceptually once sign diagrams are order automorphisms. The proof of the
p-signs needed the observation that zero sets match first (`p̃ ∘ H` and `p` vanish at the same
points; this uses Claim 1 on *both* sides, via `h.symm`), after which each root-free interval
has either a point of the Hörmander set or is unbounded (Rolle). Traps: identifiers like `p̃`
do not parse (combining tilde); `Set.mem_Iic` membership needs `Set.mem_Iic.mpr`; `push_neg` is
deprecated here (use `push Not`).
