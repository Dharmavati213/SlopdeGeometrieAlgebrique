## Plan for TameLifting (next round)

Covering statement to prove (input of `factorsPrimeTo_of_forall_isGalois`): `R` complete DVR, `k`
separably closed, `f` proper smooth geometrically connected, `Z` Galois over `X_η̄` of degree prime
to `p` ⇒ `Z ≅ E ×_X X_η̄`.
1. Descent: `Z` comes from `Z'` over `X_{K'}`, `K'/K` finite separable (template:
   `ExposeIX.exists_mono_pullback_geometricFiberι_of_lift`; X.1.8 `exists_isEquivalence_geometricPoint`
   to pass from `Ω₁` to `AlgebraicClosure K`); make `Z'` Galois by descending the automorphisms
   (`Scheme.exists_hom_of_isPullback`, `exists_map_comp_eq_of_isPullback` in
   `Foundations/Limits/FiniteEtale.lean`).
2. `V'` = integral closure of `R` in `K'`: complete DVR, residue field purely inseparable over `k`
   (needs: finite over henselian ⇒ local; Dedekind + local ⇒ DVR).
3. Core over `V'` (rename it `R`): `n = deg Z'`, `R_n = R[T]/(Tⁿ - u)` (DVR: `RootAdjunction`),
   `X_n = X ×_R R_n` regular; closed fibre irreducible with generic point `ξ`, `𝒪_{X,ξ}` a DVR with
   uniformizer `u`; at `ξ_n` the integral closure is étale by
   `ExposeXIII.etale_integralClosure_of_forall_isTamelyRamifiedOver` (tame since the Galois group has
   order prime to `p`); spread to `D(s)` (`TameLiftingAlgebra`); glue with `Z'_n` over the generic
   fibre (`Scheme.exists_isPullback_of_sup_eq_top`); extend over the codimension `≥ 2` rest by
   `AlgebraicGeometry.isEquivalence_pullback_of_isRegularLocalRing`.
4. Base change: done for `R → R_n` (`isEquivalence_pullback_adjoinRoot`); for `R → V'` apply
   `isEquivalence_pullback_baseChange_of_smooth` once `V'` is known to be a complete DVR. Assemble.
Hardest expected step: 3 (scheme ↔ ring translation at `ξ`, function field of `Z'` as a Galois
extension). Budget 2–3k lines.
