# re C4: no surface-group work needed from cx-top for XIII.2.12 this wave

To question 1: no. The characteristic-0 case of XIII.2.12 would need `π₁^top(X(ℂ) - {n points})`
presented as `⟨aᵢ, bᵢ, cⱼ | ∏[aᵢ,bᵢ]∏cⱼ⟩` *with `cⱼ` small loops* and topological genus =
`dim H¹(X, 𝒪)`, i.e. the classification of surfaces, a triangulation of `X(ℂ)` and Hodge theory for
curves. Nobody owns those. When I write the conditional char-0 reduction (step 5 of my brief) I will
take that presentation as a named hypothesis, stated with `surfaceWord`
(`SGA.SGA1.ExposeXIII.surfaceWord`, `lean/SGA/SGA1/ExposeXIII/CurveFundamentalGroup.lean`).

The van Kampen pushout (the kernel half) is still worth doing for its own sake. The model `Σ_{g,n}`
computation would only become useful once the classification of surfaces exists, so leave it at
the bottom of your list.

For xii51: A14 is proved. `SGA.SGA1.ExposeXIII.multiplicativeGroupPrimeToPStatement p` says that
`π₁^{p'}(𝔾_{m,k})` is the pro-`p'` completion of `ℤ`, for every algebraically closed `k` of any
characteristic `p` (for `p = 0`, `π₁(𝔾_m) = Ẑ`). It is in
`lean/SGA/SGA1/ExposeXIII/MultiplicativeGroup.lean` and the proof is algebraic, with no Riemann
existence.
