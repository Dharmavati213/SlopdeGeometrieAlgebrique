# EGA II 7.1.7 (finite `L`), Krull–Akizuki and IX.2.6 are proved; EGA 0_III 10.3.1 is half done

Wave 2, round 1. Everything listed here builds and is sorry-free (axioms: `propext`,
`Classical.choice`, `Quot.sound`).

## Done

- **Krull–Akizuki** (`Foundations/CommAlg/KrullAkizuki.lean`). Let `A` be a noetherian domain of
  dimension `≤ 1` and `C` a ring with `A ⊆ C ⊆ Frac A`. Then `C` is noetherian
  (`KrullAkizuki.isNoetherianRing`) and of dimension `≤ 1` (`KrullAkizuki.krullDimLE_one`). In
  valuation-ring form: `ValuationSubring.isDiscreteValuationRing_of_krullDimLE_one` (`V ≠ Frac A`)
  and, for a finite extension `L`, `ValuationSubring.isDiscreteValuationRing_of_finiteDimensional`.
  Also `Module.length_le_of_forall_fg`. The proof is Matsumura's length count `ℓ(F/aF) = ℓ(A/aA)`,
  built on mathlib's `Ring.ord` exact sequences.
- **EGA II 7.1.7** (`Foundations/CommAlg/DominatingDVR.lean`):
  - `IsLocalRing.exists_valuationSubring_isDiscreteValuationRing_of_finiteDimensional` (`L` finite
    over `K = Frac A`) and its case `L = K`, `…_isDiscreteValuationRing`;
  - xiii43's exact form, `IsLocalRing.exists_isDiscreteValuationRing_dominating`;
  - the field case included, `IsLocalRing.exists_injective_isLocalHom_isDiscreteValuationRing`
    (via `A⟦X⟧`).

  The blow-up chart is semistable's `Ideal.affineBlowupAlgebra` (row A49), so the file imports
  `SGA.Foundations.Blowup.AffineAlgebra`, which is not committed yet. The trick that keeps the
  proof short: choose `x` among generators of `𝔪` with the largest value for some valuation ring
  `V₀` dominating `A`. Then `A[𝔪/x] ⊆ V₀`, so `x` is not a unit of the chart, and no associated
  graded ring is needed.
- **Dominating points over local domains can be tested on DVRs**
  (`Foundations/CommAlg/DominatingDVRLift.lean`,
  `Algebra.exists_hasDominatingPoint_of_forall_isDiscreteValuationRing`). Take `A₀` noetherian and
  finitely many `A₀`-algebras `Bᵢ`. If every DVR over `A₀` has a point in some `Bᵢ` with values in
  a local domain dominating it, then so does every local domain `D`. The obstruction is an identity
  `1 = ∑ dⱼ cⱼ ⊗ bⱼ` in `Frac D ⊗ B` with `cⱼ ∈ 𝔪_D`. It descends to the localization of the
  `A₀`-algebra generated by the finitely many elements involved (a noetherian local domain), and
  then goes up to a DVR dominating that.

## Not done, and how I would do it

1. **IX.4.6 in SGA's form** (`IsEffectiveIffStrictlyLocalStatement`). By
   `isEffective_iff_forall_isSepClosed` (`StrictlyLocalDescent.lean`) plus faithfully flat
   descent, what is missing is this: for `R` complete noetherian local with separably closed
   residue field `k` of characteristic `p`, a flat local `R → R''` with `R''` complete noetherian
   and residue field algebraically closed (`k^{perf}`). By the completion step it is enough to
   build a non-noetherian local `C`, flat over `R`, with `𝔪_R C = 𝔪_C` and residue field
   `k^{perf}`. Plan:
   - (a) **p-bases.** Zorn gives a maximal p-independent family `(bᵢ)` (monomials with exponents
     `< p` linearly independent over `k^p`). Maximal ⇒ spanning: for `c ∉ k^p(B)`,
     `[k^p(B)(c) : k^p(B)] = p`, then use a tower basis.
   - (b) **Explicit free algebras.** `R[Yᵢ]/(Yᵢ^{pⁿ} - b̃ᵢ)` is free over `R` with the monomials of
     exponents `< pⁿ` as basis. Prove it with an explicit inverse `Ψ(X^β) = ∏ b̃ᵢ^{⌊βᵢ/pⁿ⌋} e_{β mod pⁿ}`
     that kills the ideal. Its special fibre is a field (from (a), with `pⁿ`-independence by
     induction), it is local with maximal ideal `𝔪_R C`, and its residue field is `k^{1/pⁿ}`.
   - (c) **Sequential colimit** over `n` (`Yᵢ ↦ Yᵢ^p`), with `Ring.DirectLimit` over `ℕ`. It is
     flat (directed colimit of flat), local, `𝔪_R C = 𝔪_C`, and its residue field is `k^{perf}`.
   - Then complete, and conclude IX.4.6 with
     `DescentDatum.isEffective_of_isEffective_baseChange_of_flat_of_isSeparated`.

   The general A42 (any `K`) needs either a transfinite tower of monogenic steps (mathlib's
   `SuccStruct` iteration in some category of local rings over `K`) or transcendental, separable
   and p-basis steps. IX.4.6 needs only `k^{perf}`.
2. **EGA II 7.1.7 for transcendental `L`** (`IsLocalRing.DominatingDVRStatement`). Take a
   transcendence basis `x` (finite: `FinTrdeg` from `EssFiniteType`). Set
   `A₁ = (MvPolynomial ι A)_{(𝔪, X)}`, embedded in `L` by `aeval x`; it is injective by algebraic
   independence and `MvPolynomial.map_injective`. Show `L` finite over `Frac A₁` (algebraic, and
   essentially of finite type over it), then apply
   `exists_valuationSubring_isDiscreteValuationRing_of_finiteDimensional` to `A₁`. Nobody consumes
   this; estimate 200 lines of field plumbing.
