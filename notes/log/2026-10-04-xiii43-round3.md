  (`ExposeX/TameLiftingProof.lean`). This is the X.3.8 core (SGA's X.3.7 target), for the actual map
  `π₁(X_η̄) → π₁(X)`. `R` is a complete DVR with separably closed residue field.
- **`SGA.SGA1.ExposeXIII.properSmoothHomotopyExactSequence_of_isDiscreteValuationRing`**
  (`ExposeXIII/ProperSmoothTame.lean`). The second part of XIII.4.4 for `S = Spec R`, with `R` as
  above, at every geometric point and without the section. At the generic point it comes from the
  core (`isProLShortExact_generic_of_isSepClosed_residueField`), at the closed point from X.2.1.
  The group step is `comap_primeKernel_le_proLKernel_of_factorsPrimeTo`: `FactorsPrimeTo u q`, with
  no prime of `L` dividing `q`, gives injectivity of `π₁^L(X_s̄) → π'₁(X)`.
- **`SGA.SGA1.ExposeX.exists_tameSpecialization_of_isDiscreteValuationRing`**
  (`ExposeX/TameLiftingSpecialization.lean`). In-scope X.3.8, existence form, for `Y = Spec R` with
  `R` as above, `y₀` the closed and `y₁` the generic point:
  `sp = i₀⁻¹ ∘ (path) ∘ i₁`, where `i₀ : π₁(X_{b₀}) → π₁(X)` is bijective (X.2.1 for normal `X` and
  X.1.4). X.3.9 follows for these bases: `exists_primeToQuotientEquiv_of_isDiscreteValuationRing`,
  and `exists_bijective_specialization_of_isDiscreteValuationRing` in characteristic 0.
- Interfaces (statements only):

## How the core is assembled (new files, all in `ExposeX/`)

- `TameLiftingDescent.lean`:
  - `LiftsEndos H Y` (every endomorphism of `H Y` comes from `Y`), with `of_full`, `of_iso`,
    `of_natIso`, `comp`, `of_comp`;
  - `isConnected_of_isConnected_obj` and `isGalois_of_liftsEndos`: a fibre-compatible functor
    reflects connectedness, and `Y` is Galois if `H Y` is and `LiftsEndos H Y`;
  - `natCard_aut_eq_of_isGalois`;
  - `exists_liftsEndos_of_isLimit`: the limit theorem for étale coverings, with their finitely
    many endomorphisms, at a common level. It uses `Scheme.exists_hom_of_isPullback` once per
    endomorphism and `IsCofiltered.inf_objs_exists` in `Over j`.
- `TameLiftingFiniteLevel.lean`: `exists_isGalois_finiteDimensional_of_isGalois`. A Galois `Z` over
  `X_Ω` is `α^* Y₀` for a Galois `Y₀` over `X_{K₀} = X ×_R K₀` with `|Aut Y₀| = |Aut Z|`, where
  `K₀/K` is finite separable and `α = pullbackMapOfIsScalarTower`. The steps are X.1.8 to `K̄ ⊆ Ω`,
  then the limit over `FiniteSubext K K̄`, then IX.4.10 along `X_L ⟶ X_{K₀}` (template:
  `ExposeIX.exists_mono_pullback_geometricFiberι_of_lift`). Galois-ness comes once, at the end, from
  `isGalois_of_liftsEndos` for the composite functor.
- `TameLiftingProof.lean`:
  - `exists_iso_pullback_of_isGalois_of_finiteDimensional`, with `V₀ = integralClosure R K₀`:
    - `isLocalization_away_of_irreducible` identifies the generic fibre `U₀` of `X_{V₀}` with
      `X_{K₀}`, via `IsOpenImmersion.isPullback`;
    - round 2's `exists_iso_pullback_of_isGalois_of_isAdicComplete` extends over `V₀`;
    - `isEquivalence_pullback_baseChange_of_smooth` descends to `X`;
    - a root of `Tⁿ - π₀` in `Ω` gives `γ : X_Ω ⟶ U₀ₙ`, which identifies the inverse images.
  - The final theorem then follows from `factorsPrimeTo_of_forall_isGalois`.

## What was hard, and why

1. **`set x := e with h` on a term inside an instance-carrying type.** `set s := Spec.map ...`
   created a second `Z` whose type mentions `s`, but `[IsGalois Z]` still referred to the old `Z`.
   Use `let`.
2. **`rw [hs]` where `s` also occurs in `pullback.snd f s`** rewrites inside the type (motive
   error). Use `(pullback.condition).trans (congrArg (pullback.snd f s ≫ ·) hs)`.
3. **simp does not see through let-bound morphisms in pullbacks.** `pullback.lift_fst` did not fire on
   `pullback.lift … ≫ pullback.fst h ιL` with `ιL` a `let`; `erw` did.
4. **`IsPullback.of_right'` builds its own lift.** Use `IsPullback.of_right o hcomm t` to keep your
   morphism.
5. **`K̄` (combining macron) is not a Lean identifier.** The parser says "expected token". Use `Kb`.
6. `change … ∈ _` with an `Opens` on the right gets stuck on `Membership ?m`. Ascribe the type
   `(… : (Spec _).Opens)`.
7. The descent of the automorphisms was the only conceptual gap left after round 2. Proving
   "every endomorphism lifts" (`LiftsEndos`) instead of descending a group action kept it at about
   100 lines.
