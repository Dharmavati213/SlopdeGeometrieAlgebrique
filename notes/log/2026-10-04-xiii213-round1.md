## Next (exact plan for the rest of Serre's theorem)

Goal: prove `AffineLinePExtensionStatement` (then `abhyankarAffineLine_of_cases` needs only
patching and case B). Induct on `|G|`: pick `M` minimal normal in `G` inside the `p`-kernel; it is
elementary abelian (`Z(M)` and `Mᵖ` are characteristic); `G/M` is realized by induction. The weak
lift `exists_lift_of_elementary_ker` has image `I` with `I·M = G`, and `I ∩ M` is normal, so
either the lift is onto or `I` is a complement (split case). Central `M`: use the R3 twist
(`exists_surjective_lift_of_central`). Split, non-central `M`:
1. For `a ∈ (B ⊗ M)^H` (invariants), the cocycle `u(v·s(σ)) = v` (s the complement) satisfies the
   hypotheses of `vasSemilinearAut` with this `a`; it gives a lift `ψ_a`.
2. If `ψ_a` is not onto, `ker ψ ⊆ ker ψ_a`, so the point of `B'_a` has stabilizer containing that
   of the point of `B`; by `exists_hom_of_stabilizer_le` there is a `k[T]`-map `B'_a → B`, which is
   `B`-linear after composing with an element of `H` (endomorphisms of the connected Galois
   object), so `a ∈ ℘(B ⊗ M)`.
3. Existence of `a ∈ (B⊗M)^H ∖ ℘(B⊗M)`: apply `exists_mem_forall_ne_add_sub` to `W = Bʳ` (Pi
   basis of a `k[T]`-basis of `B`; `B` is free since finite projective over a PID), `F` the
   coordinatewise Frobenius (bounds from `exists_deg_pow_char_bounds_pi`, discriminant unit from
   `ExposeI.etale_iff_isUnit_discr`), `M = (Bʳ)^H` (nonzero via the Chase–Harrison–Rosenberg
   elements `ExposeV.exists_galois_elements`: `∑ xᵢ Tr(yᵢ v) = v`), and `S₀` = one representative
   of `M ∩ ℘(W)` per function `H → (ℤ/p)ʳ`, `σ ↦ σy - y` (`ker ℘ = 𝔽_p` coordinates in the
   domain `B`).
Then `exists_surjective_of_pKernel` and the transport to `FundamentalGroup x` as in R3.
