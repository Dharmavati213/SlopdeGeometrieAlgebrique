## What was hard, and why

- I had planned to prove flatness of `A^sh → B^sh` by identifying `B^sh` with a strict
  henselization of `(A^sh ⊗_A B)_q` (hundreds of lines). It is 25 lines: mathlib's
  `Algebra.FormallyUnramified.flat_of_restrictScalars` (Iversen I.2.7) + the existing colimit lemma
  `StrictHenselization.flat_of_forall_flat`. Lesson in strategy.md.
- `set ν := f.fromNormalization` broke instance search (LocallyOfFiniteType, QuasiCompact,
  Surjective of `ν`) — fixed with `local notation` (strategy.md).
- Points of curves: `Spec A_t` charts instead of open subschemes made zero loci and subschemes
  computable (strategy.md).
