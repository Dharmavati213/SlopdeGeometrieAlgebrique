- The general tool behind it (VII 5.7 in degree 0, surjectivity half):
  `Scheme.exists_toLimitSections_eq F (t := t) hp hc σ`, for a cofiltered `E : I ⥤ Scheme`
  (qcqs pieces, affine transitions), `t : E ⟶ (Functor.const I).obj X` with every `t.app k`
  étale, a limit cone `c`, `hp : ∀ k, c.π.app k ≫ t.app k = p`: every
  `σ ∈ Γ(c.pt, p^* F)` is `toLimitSections F t hp k s` for some `k`, `s ∈ F(Etale.mk (t.app k))`.
- Reusable helpers: `exists_forall_pullback_fst_notMem` (a closed subset of `T`, `T ⟶ E j` qc,
  missing `T ×_{E j} c.pt` misses some `T ×_{E j} E k`; Stacks 01Z3 via mathlib's
  `exists_mem_of_isClosed_of_nonempty` on the existing `Scheme.baseChangeDiagram`),
  `isLimitPullbackDiagramCone` (`W ×_X c.pt` is the limit of `W ×_X E k`),
  `exists_mem_etaleAgreementLocus_etaleAdjunction_unit` (a section of `p^* F` agrees near any

xiii3 (A31): use `Scheme.Hom.bijective_pushforwardStalkToStrictLocalization` directly.

**Technique that worked.** "Local models" `(φ : Q ⟶ E k, b ∈ F(Q))` with a predicate
`AgreesWith` (the images of `b` and `σ` agree on `c.pt ×_{E k} Q`), stable under
`AgreesWith.comp` (any `ψ : Q' ⟶ Q` over a lower level). Overlaps are handled pointwise: from
the closed-set descent we get "every point of `T ×_{E k₂} E m` maps into the agreement locus",
which survives composing `m ⟶ k₂` with anything; a common level for finitely many pairs is taken
in `Over k₂` (`IsCofiltered.inf_objs_exists`), so the triangles commute for free. Traps:
`rw` with goals over `Etale` objects fails ("motive is not type correct"): use
`(congrArg (· ∈ O) h).mpr`, `e₁.trans (congrArg _ h)`; a `let`-bound section family gets
unfolded by `rw [← Functor.map_comp_apply]`, so make it opaque with
`obtain ⟨b₂, hb₂⟩ : ∃ b₂, … := ⟨_, …⟩`; `pullback.fst _ _ x` needs explicit arguments inside
`congrArg`; never `cat > file` without a heredoc in a compound command (it waits on stdin).
