  - refuting that needs `T_ℓ(B) ≠ 0`, i.e. `deg ℓ_B > 1`, i.e. degree theory again.

## 2. New: SGA's `T(A) = ∏_ℓ T_ℓ(A)` and the equivalence (`TateModuleProduct.lean`)

- `tateModuleEquivPi : T(G) ≃ₜ* ∏_{ℓ prime} T_ℓ(G)`, for any commutative group object.
  - The inverse's `n`-th component is the product over the primes `ℓ ∣ n` of the `n`-th components
    of `tateModuleOfAt (z_ℓ)`.
  - Injectivity: `eq_one_of_forall_tateModuleToAt_eq_one`, an order argument with
    `Nat.not_dvd_ordCompl`.
- General facts on compact commutative groups (`TateModulePrimary.lean`):
  - `isClosed_primaryComponent`;
  - `exists_mem_primaryComponent_inv_mul_mem`: an element with `σ^{ℓ^v} ∈ U` is `ℓ`-primary
    modulo `U`, by finite intersections and compactness;
  - `exists_mem_inv_mul_mem_of_primaryComponent_subset`: a subgroup containing all primary
    components is dense, by Bézout induction on `N` with `σ^N ∈ U`.
- The equivalence goes through `exists_tateModule_equiv_of_isPretransitive`:
  - compactness, since `T_ℓ` is homeomorphic to a closed set;
  - faithfulness, from `exists_coe_tateModuleOfAt_tateModuleToAt_apply_eq_pow`: the components of
    the `ℓ`-primary part of `x` are powers of those of `x`;
  - transitivity, from density.

## 3. New: XI.2's statement before XI.2.1, connected case (`AbelianVarietyCovering.lean`)

SGA: a pointed principal covering with commutative group carries a unique group law making it a
homomorphism, and if connected it is an abelian variety isogenous to `A`.

- `existsUnique_grpObj_coveringOver`: for a connected étale covering `p : A' ⟶ A` and `0'` over
  `0`, there is a unique `GrpObj` on `A'` with origin `0'` and `p` a homomorphism.
- `A'` is proper and smooth (instances). `p` is finite and surjective (`isFinite_coveringHom`,
  `surjective_coveringHom`).
- `exists_isMonHom_comp_eq_mulN`: the Serre–Lang lift of `n_A` is a homomorphism. This is SGA's
  "every isogeny is a quotient of some `n_A`", for these coverings.
- Method:
  - lift `μ` (`exists_mul_lift`), using Künneth X.1.7: `π₁(A' × A')` is generated by the axes;
  - lift `ι` (`exists_inv_lift`), using `pointedMap_inv`: `ι_*` is inversion;
  - prove the axioms by rigidity of lifts (`coveringHom_ext`) on `A'`, `A' ⊗ A'` and
    `(A' ⊗ A') ⊗ A'`, which are connected (`connectedSpace_tensor`, from X.1.5);
  - assemble with `GrpObj.ofRepresentableBy` (technique in `strategy.md`).
- Not done: disconnected principal coverings, i.e. `Ext(A, G) ≅ H¹(A, G)`.
