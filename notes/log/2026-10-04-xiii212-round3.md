**A16: `PrimeToPCoveringsTameStatement` is proved.** The file is
`CurveFundamentalGroupTame.lean`, about 430 lines.
- `primeToPCoveringsTameStatement : PrimeToPCoveringsTameStatement` (`tameKernel ≤ proLKernel p'`).
- As a consequence, `tameCurvePrimeToPStatement_of_tameCurveFundamentalGroupStatement'`: XIII.2.12
  implies its "in other words" form with no extra hypothesis.
- The chain of proof:
  - `GaloisCoveringsTameStatement` (new, statement): a Galois covering of `U` of degree prime to
    `p` is tamely ramified along `X - U`. It is proved as `galoisCoveringsTameStatement`.
  - With V.5.11, it gives the result:
    `primeToPCoveringsTameStatement_of_galoisCoveringsTameStatement`. `U` is connected because
    `X` is integral (A19, `ExposeX.isIntegral_and_isNormalScheme_of_smooth`) and `ξ` gives a
    point.
  - The algebra (namespace `TameGaloisAlgebra`):
    - `isGalois_quotient_of_bijective`: let a group `S` of `K`-automorphisms of a finite
      unramified `K`-algebra `A` act simply transitively on `A →ₐ[K] Ω`. Then each residue field
      `A ⧸ m` is Galois over `K`, of degree `|Stab(m)|`, which divides `|S|`. The proof counts:
      `Stab(m) ≃ ((A ⧸ m) →ₐ[K] Ω)`, and `Stab(m) ↪ Gal(A ⧸ m / K)` by
      `Ideal.quotientEquivAlg`.
    - `isTameExtension_of_isGalois`: a Galois extension of degree prime to the residue
      characteristic is tame over any local `R`, with no DVR assumption. It uses
      `finrank_galoisClosure`: the Galois closure of a normal `L` is `L`, by
      `AlgHom.fieldRange_of_normal`.
    - `isTameAlgebra_of_bijective` puts the two together.
  - The geometry:
    - `pullbackAlgebra E κ` is the algebra of `E ×_U Spec K`, obtained through
      `specEquivalence`.
    - `autHom : Aut E →* Aut_K(pullbackAlgebra)`.
    - `bijective_comp_range`: simple transitivity, transported from
      `evaluation_aut_bijective_of_isGalois` along
      `fiberIso : F_{η≫κ} ≅ FEt.pullback κ ⋙ geometricFiber K Ω`. The key identity
      `comp_autHom` holds by `rfl`.
    - `etalePullbackYonedaIso`: the sheaf `κ^* h_E` is `yoneda (etaleSpec K L)`. It is built
      from the repo's `Scheme.etalePullbackYonedaIso` and the counit of `specEquivalence`.
- Note that the proof uses no property of the boundary point `y` beyond its local ring
  being local and an `Ω'`-algebra (residue characteristic = `char k`). A Galois `p'`-covering is
  tame at every point.

## Hard, and why

- **No isomorphism `Γ(U) ≅ k[T, T⁻¹]` in the statement.** I first took `ψ : Γ(U) ≃+* k[T, T⁻¹]`
  with `ψ(t) = T` as a hypothesis. For `𝔸¹` this needed mathlib's instance
  `Algebra R Γ(Spec R, U)`. Instance search does not find it for `R = .of k[X]` and
  `U = PrimeSpectrum.basicOpen X`, even with `backward.isDefEq.respectTransparency.types false`.
  - The fix was to build `ψ` from `φ` with `IsLocalization.ringEquivOfRingEquiv` and
    `hV.isLocalization_basicOpen`, whose algebra `algebra_section_section_basicOpen` is found
    without trouble. Pass `T := Submonoid.powers X` explicitly, or `rw [Submonoid.map_powers]`
    closes the goal by unifying `T` with the wrong submonoid.
- **`U` as a variable.** The torus of `ℙ¹` is `basicOpen chartCoord₀`, `basicOpen chartCoord₁` and
  `projectiveSpace.torus` at once. The chart theorem therefore takes
  `hU : X.basicOpen (φ.symm X) = U` and does `subst hU` first. `homOfLE` with different proofs is
  defeq, so the restriction maps match after the substitution.
- **`local notation "ℙ¹"` inside `variable`** gives "Unknown constant `k✝`". Write
  `Proj (MvPolynomial.homogeneousSubmodule (Fin 2) k)` there.
- **`include`.** A lemma whose statement does not mention `hxV` but whose proof uses it needs
  `include hxV in`. Otherwise the error is "Unknown identifier `hxV`", or an application type
  mismatch at the call site, because the explicit arguments shift.

## Hard, and why (A16)

- **Write the simple transitivity in the algebra, not in a Galois category.** The categorical
  route through connected components of `κ^* E` in `FEt(Spec K)` looked shorter, but the algebraic
  version (`S ≤ Aut_K(A)` simply transitive on `A →ₐ[K] Ω`) needs only counting and
  `Ideal.quotientEquivAlg`.
- **Getting from the Galois category to the algebra was cheap**, because
  `geometricFiber R Ω` is by definition `specEquivalence.inverse ⋙ fiberFunctor`, and
  `fiberFunctor.map f φ = φ.comp f.unop.hom.hom` (mathlib `FiniteEtale.fiber`). Then
  `autOpMulEquivAlgEquiv` (xiii213) with `MulEquiv.inv'` turns `Functor.mapAut` into a
  homomorphism to `Aut_K(A)`, and the action identity is `rfl`.
- **A FintypeCat iso as a bijection:** `(FintypeCat.equivEquivIso.symm (α.app E)).bijective`
  matches `⇑(α.app E).hom`.
- **`MorphismProperty.Over.isoMk`** from an iso in another `MorphismProperty.Over` (FEt to Etale):
  push it to `Over` with `(MorphismProperty.Over.forget _ _ _ ⋙ Over.forget _).mapIso`, and prove
  the triangle with
  `by exact Over.w ((MorphismProperty.Over.forget _ ⊤ _).map c.inv)`.
- A `set F := A ⧸ m` loses the `Algebra K F` instance. Write `A ⧸ m` everywhere instead.
