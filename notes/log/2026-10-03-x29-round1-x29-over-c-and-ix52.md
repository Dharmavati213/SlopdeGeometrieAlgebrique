## The critic's "major" objection (special vs generic hyperplane)

Settled, in Lean (`topologicallyFiniteStatement_of_curve_of_hyperplane`, hypothesis `hH`): the
hyperplane step is taken in **existence form**: for `X` normal integral projective of dim ≥ 2 over `k = k̄`, there are an
algebraically closed `K ⊇ k` and a closed `Y ⊆ X_K`, proper, connected, `dim Y < dim X`, with
`π₁(Y) ↠ π₁(X_K)`. SGA's special-hyperplane X.2.10 implies it with `K = k`; the generic
hyperplane (Bertini field lemma over `K = alg. closure of k(u)`) implies it too. With X.1.8
(`π₁(X_K) ≅ π₁(X)`, proved) the induction runs over all algebraically closed fields, so either
proof of the step can be plugged in. In char 0 this keeps fields of cardinality 𝔠 (≅ ℂ), so the
curve case over ℂ already covers the base of the induction there.
