**`MultiplicativeGroupInertiaKummer.lean`** completes A14 at `0`.

## Hard, and why

- **Universes.** `π₁` lives in `Type (u+1)`, but A14's bijectivity quantifies over finite groups in
  `Type u`. The quotients `π₁ ⧸ N` therefore cannot be fed back into A14. The fix is
  `bijective_eval_of_small`, which goes through `Shrink.{u}` and `Shrink.mulEquiv`.
- **The coercion between `PrimeSpectrum.basicOpen r` and `(Spec R).Opens`.**
  - `Scheme.isoOfEq_inv_ι` and `isoOfEq_hom_ι` do not `rw`, because the motive is not
    type-correct. State the lemma with `Scheme.Opens.ι (X := Spec _) (PrimeSpectrum.basicOpen r)`
    and use `erw`.
  - `basicOpenIsoSpecAway X` needs `(R := .of k[X])` written out.
- **`IsIso (pullback.fst f e.hom)` for an iso `e` is sometimes not found by instance search.**
  Write `pullback_fst_iso_of_right_iso _ _` and pass it to `@asIso`.
- **Name clash.** Inside `namespace SGA.SGA1.ExposeXIII`, the name `fiberFunctor` resolves to
  `ExposeXIII.fiberFunctor`, so you have to write `ExposeV.fiberFunctor`.
- **`Scheme.Hom.appLE` does not unfold under the coercion.** State
  `f.appLE ⊤ V le_top x = X.presheaf.map (homOfLE _).op (f.appTop x)` as `rfl` and rewrite with it.
- **`HasRingHomProperty.of_iSup_eq_top`.** Its `Q` for `SmoothOfRelativeDimension` is
  `RingHom.Locally (IsStandardSmoothOfRelativeDimension n)`. Use `RingHom.locally_of` with
  `isStandardSmoothOfRelativeDimension_respectsIso`.
