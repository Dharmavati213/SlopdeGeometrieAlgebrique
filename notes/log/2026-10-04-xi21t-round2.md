## 2. XI.2.1 in every characteristic, from SGA's cited input

`SGA1/ExposeXI/AbelianVarietyQuotient.lean`:

  AbelianVarietyFundamentalGroupStatement`. `MulNIsogenyStatement` ("`n_A` is an isogeny") is the
  fact SGA itself only recalls.
- `exists_tateModule_equiv_of_charP`: in characteristic `p`, only `p_A` finite and surjective is
  assumed. For `n` prime to `p` this is `mulNIsogeny_of_ne_zero` (`AbelianVarietyMulN.lean`).

The missing piece was an étale covering through which `n_A` lifts injectively on `K_n`
(`exists_lift_mulN_injective`). It is built entirely from repo tools:

- `K_n` acts on `A` by translations (`translate`). The quotient `A/K_n` is taken relative to the
  finite `n_A` (V.1.8, `ExposeV.quotientScheme`, so no admissibility argument is needed).
- Inertia is trivial (`eq_one_of_mem_inertiaGroup_translate`: a translation fixing
  `Spec κ(x) ⟶ A` is trivial in the group `A(κ(x))`), so `A ⟶ A/K_n` is étale (V.2.3).
- `A/K_n ⟶ A` is finite. It is radicial (`universallyInjective_fromQuotient_translate`), through
  mathlib's `tfae_universallyInjective`:
  - pass to `Spec Ω̄` (an epimorphism);
  - lift geometric points along the étale quotient map;
  - two `Ω`-points with the same image under `n_A` differ by an element of `K_n`
    (`exists_torsionPoints_comp_eq`): `a₁a₂⁻¹` lands in the finite fibre over `0`, so its point is
    closed (`Scheme.Hom.isClosed_singleton_of_isClosed_singleton_apply`), and an `Ω`-point at a
    closed point comes from a `k`-point (`AlgebraicGeometry.eq_comp_pointOfClosedPoint`, Points.lean).
- IX.4.10 for étale coverings (`ExposeIX.essSurj_fetPullback_of_isFinite`) descends `A ⟶ A/K_n` to
  `Y ⟶ A`, and `A ≅ Y ×_A A/K_n ⟶ Y` is the injective lift.

## 4. The `ℓ`-primary clause for `ℓ ≠ char k`, unconditionally

`SGA1/ExposeXI/TateModulePrimeToP.lean`: `abelianVarietyPrimaryComponent_of_isUnit`. For every prime
`ℓ` invertible in `k`, the `ℓ`-primary component of `π₁(A)` is canonically `T_ℓ(A)`, with no input
on `p_A`. XI.2.1 is therefore open only for the `p`-primary component in characteristic `p`. The
isomorphism is `ψ = φ ∘ tateModuleOfAt`, where `φ` is the canonical map, which exists in every
characteristic.

- **Injective** (`injective_tateModuleToFundamentalGroup_comp_tateModuleOfAt`): `K_{ℓ^r}` acts
  faithfully on the étale covering `(ℓ^r)_A`. `T_ℓ` is compact (`compactSpace_tateModuleAt`), so
  `ψ` is a closed embedding.
- **Onto the `ℓ`-primary part.** The range is closed. By mathlib's `nhds_one_has_basis_stabilizers`
  it suffices to match an `ℓ`-primary `σ` at one point `y` of any covering `Y`
  (`exists_tateModuleAt_smul_eq`):
  - Serre–Lang gives `N = ℓ^a m` with `ℓ ∤ m`.
  - Pull `Y` back along `m_A`. There `π₁` acts by `τ ↦ τ^m` (`pointedMap_mulN`), and `(g, (ℓ^a)_A)`
    lifts `(ℓ^a)_A`.
  - On the connected component of `y''` the lift exists by the pointed criterion. Its hypothesis
    comes from the converse of that criterion, `pointedMap_smul_eq_of_lift`: a section from the
    terminal covering.
  - `exists_torsionPoints_of_lift` gives `c ∈ K_{ℓ^a}` with `σ y'' = g'(c)`.
  - `c` lifts to `z ∈ T_ℓ` by successive `ℓ`-th roots (`exists_tateModuleAt_apply_eq`; no
    finiteness needed).
  - Then `(ψ z)^m` and `σ^m` agree at `y`. With `δ = σ⁻¹ ψ z`, both `δ^m` and some `δ^{ℓ^b}` lie in
    the stabilizer of `y` (`π₁` commutative: `mul_comm_of_monObj`), so `δ` does too, by Bézout
    (`mem_of_pow_mem_of_coprime`).
