# Foundations

Prerequisites of the SGA 1 formalization that mathlib does not have. They are written as if for
mathlib: mathlib namespaces and naming, no SGA numbers in names, and module docstrings citing EGA,
SGA 4 or the Stacks Project. The barrel is `SGA.Foundations` (`SGA/Foundations.lean`), whose
docstring lists the areas covered.

## Out of scope

Some results of SGA 1 rest on theories that the seminar quotes from elsewhere: Hodge theory,
resolution of singularities, complex analytic geometry beyond the local theory, the étale
cohomology of SGA 4, Raynaud's proof of Abhyankar's conjecture. They were first recorded here as
faithful `Prop`-valued `…Statement` definitions, with every consequence SGA draws from them proved
from the statement. A campaign started on 2026-10-03 is formalizing them; the shared prerequisites
each have one owner, listed in [`notes/topics/out-of-scope-plan.md`](../../../notes/topics/out-of-scope-plan.md).
Some routes avoid the theory SGA quotes: X.2.9 in characteristic 0 (for `#k ≤ 𝔠`, in universe 0)
is proved without Riemann existence; XII.5.2 is reduced to XII.5.1 without triangulation; a route
to XIII.4.6 that avoids resolution of singularities reduces the normal case to
`AffineLineOpenInvarianceStatement`. Of the statements in the second column, only XIII.3.2 1)
(`FieldCohomologicalPropernessStatement`) is proved in full. The third column lists partial and
conditional results with their hypotheses.

### Wave 2 (2026-10-04)

The modules added in wave 2 are imported from `SGA.Foundations` (and, where the statement lives in
an exposé, from that exposé's barrel). The declarations below are theorems in the tree. What they
do not prove is named as a `…Statement` definition.

* **Analytic.** Oka's coherence theorem, germ form: `AnalyticGeometry.okaCoherence :
  OkaCoherenceStatement` (`Analytic/Oka.lean`). Theorem B for `𝒪` on
  `Δ(r) × ℂᵃ × (ℂ*)ᵇ`: `AnalyticGeometry.polydiscProductVanishing :
  PolydiscProductVanishingStatement` (`Analytic/TheoremB.lean`). The same directory has Runge
  approximation on compact boxes, the Hartogs extension in dimension `≥ 2`, and Dolbeault's lemma
  on products of open sets in `ℂ`. Still open: `CartanSerreFinitenessStatement` and
  `CoherentTheoremABStatement` (`Analytic/CoherentStatements.lean`).
* **Commutative algebra.** EGA II 7.1.7: `IsLocalRing.dominatingDVRStatement`
  (`CommAlg/DominatingDVRGeneral.lean`). EGA 0_III 10.3.1:
  `IsLocalRing.flatResidueExtensionStatement` (`CommAlg/FlatResidueExtensionGeneral.lean`).
  Krull–Akizuki for a ring between `A` and a finite extension of `Frac A`:
  `isNoetherianRing_of_finiteDimensional` and `krullDimLE_one_of_finiteDimensional`
  (`CommAlg/KrullAkizukiFinite.lean`).
* **Limits.** EGA IV 8.8.2: `Scheme.spreadingOutStatement` (`Limits/SpreadingOutGluing.lean`).
  EGA IV 8.10.5 for proper morphisms: `Scheme.properLimitStatement`
  (`Limits/PropertiesLimitProper.lean`). Spreading out to a subfield:
  `Scheme.spreadingOutSubfieldStatement` (`Limits/SpreadingOutSubfield.lean`).
* **Hodge.** The language of `(p, q)`-forms, `∂` and `∂̄`, and the open statements
  `Hodge.CompactKahlerHodgeSymmetryStatement` and `Hodge.DolbeaultIsomorphismStatement`
  (`Hodge/`). Hodge symmetry is not proved, so XI.1.4 stays reduced to it.
* **Curves, blow-ups, semistable reduction.** A smooth proper curve over a field is finite and
  flat over `ℙ¹`: `AlgebraicGeometry.smoothProperCurveFiniteFlatStatement`
  (`Projective/CurveProjective.lean` states it; the proof is
  `SGA.SGA1.ExposeIII.CurveLiftCurve`). `Semistable/` and `Blowup/` hold the numerical types and
  blow-ups used toward semistable reduction. `SemistableReductionStatement` is still open.
* **Topology and projective geometry.** `Topology/Surface*` is the topology of compact surfaces
  (presentations, Schreier transversals). `Projective/Bertini*` and `Projective/PlaneModel*` are
  the hyperplane and plane-model steps used for X.2.9. `BranchedCoveringPresentationStatement`
  (`Topology/SurfaceBranchedCovering.lean`) is still a definition.
